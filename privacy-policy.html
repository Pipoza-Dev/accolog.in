Privacy Policy

1. Overview & Data Philosophy

Accolog ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed and maintained by PipoZa Dev Studio. Accolog is an offline-first digital customer ledger, khata notebook, and business record management utility designed for small retail shopkeepers, local store owners, and independent merchants.

Our core principle is User Data Sovereignty: all customer records, reference numbers, transaction notes, and due balances entered into the app belong exclusively to you. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or train machine learning models on your business data.

🛡️ The Accolog Commitment: Your customer ledger is stored locally on your device's private, sandboxed internal storage and can be backed up exclusively to your own personal Google Drive account. We do not run third-party surveillance trackers or advertising networks.

2. Information We Handle

Accolog operates on a minimal-data model. The information handled includes:

  • User Profile Information: Store name, operator name, and registered email address provided when you set up the app or sign in.
  • Customer Records Entered by You: Customer Name, Reference ID/Account Number, Mobile Phone Number, Custom Category/Status Tags, Due Balances, and Transaction Notes. All customer entries are stored locally on your phone's internal storage database.
  • Customer Photos (Optional): If you choose to attach portrait pictures or customer receipt images, photos are cropped and saved directly in your device's private storage directory.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Anonymous crash reports and error logs collected strictly to resolve software bugs and optimize performance.

3. Device Permissions & Rationale

Accolog requests only standard operating system permissions that are strictly necessary to provide core app features:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely when you choose to take a photo of a customer for their ledger profile. The camera is never accessed in the background and is marked optional (android:required="false").
  • Photos & Media Access (android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Allows you to select profile photos or receipts from your gallery, and export customer account slips or backup files.
  • Biometric Authentication (android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT): Allows you to lock and unlock the app using your device's hardware fingerprint sensor or face recognition. Biometric validation is processed entirely on-device by the Android operating system. No biometric data is ever stored, accessed, or transmitted by Accolog.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Enables the app to deliver scheduled task alerts, customer payment follow-ups, and backup status notifications.
  • Exact Alarms (android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM): Allows the app to trigger reminder notifications at the exact time you schedule for customer due dates or store follow-up appointments.
  • Boot Completed (android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Automatically restores and reschedules your active due reminders if your phone is restarted.
  • Internet & Network State (android.permission.INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used exclusively to check network connectivity, check for newer app updates, and sync optional backups directly to your personal Google Drive account.

4. Local Offline-First Storage Architecture

Accolog functions completely offline without requiring continuous internet connectivity. All customer ledger records, notes, and photos are saved in a private, sandboxed internal storage directory on your physical device.

Other applications installed on your phone cannot read, modify, or access your Accolog ledger database. You retain full control to view, edit, search, and wipe records locally at any time.

5. Private Google Drive Cloud Backups

Accolog provides an optional, user-initiated backup feature that connects directly to your own personal Google Drive account:

  • Backups are uploaded directly between your device and Google's official Drive API over secure TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • Accolog saves backup archives in a dedicated, isolated app data folder within your personal Google account.
  • Accolog does not access, view, or modify any other files, photos, or documents in your Google Drive.
  • The developer (PipoZa Dev Studio) has zero access to your Google Drive credentials or your backup files.

6. Zero Third-Party Advertising & No Data Selling

We believe business ledgers must remain private and distraction-free:

  • Accolog does not contain third-party advertising SDKs (no AdMob, Meta Ads, or tracking beacons).
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your customer phone numbers, names, or due amounts with advertisers, data brokers, or marketing companies.

7. User Control & Data Deletion Policy

In compliance with Google Play User Data policies and the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, you have absolute authority over your data:

  • Export Data: You can export customer records and summaries locally or share digital slips directly from the app at any time.
  • Local Data Deletion: You can edit or delete individual customer accounts, transaction notes, or photos at any time. You can also wipe all local app data instantly through App Settings > Clear Data or via your Android device system settings.
  • Account & Cloud Deletion: If you use the optional cloud backup feature and wish to delete your associated records, you can delete backup archives directly from your Google Drive storage or email us at mandalpiush720@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 48 to 72 business hours.

8. Security Safeguards

We implement industry-standard technical and operational safeguards:

  • On-device biometric locking (fingerprint and face authentication) to protect your ledger from unauthorized bystanders.
  • End-to-end transport encryption (TLS 1.3) during version checks and Google Drive backup synchronization.
  • Android OS sandboxed internal storage preventing external apps from reading ledger records.

9. Important Service Disclaimer

⚠️ Non-Banking & Non-Financial Disclaimer:
Accolog is an independent, offline customer ledger and digital khata notebook designed strictly for local shopkeepers, merchants, and retail businesses for record-keeping and bookkeeping.

Accolog is NOT a bank, financial institution, non-banking financial company (NBFC), payment gateway, money lender, or government entity. The app does not facilitate online fund transfers, process payments, issue loans, provide investment advice, or offer official banking services.
